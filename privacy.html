Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HRPro ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website located at https://hr-management-system-nine.vercel.app.

1. Information We Collect

When you apply for a job or use our platform, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and address.
  • Professional Information: CV/Resume, work experience, education, and skills.
  • Assessment Data: Answers provided during online assessments.

2. How We Use Google User Data (Gmail API)

Our application uses the Google Gmail API to send transactional emails. Specifically, we request the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send scope.

  • What we access: We only access the ability to send emails from the authorized Gmail account. We do not read, delete, or modify your existing inbox, contacts, or any other Google user data.
  • How we use it: The Gmail API is used solely to send automated notification emails to job applicants (e.g., application receipts, assessment invitations, offer letters) and employees (e.g., payslips).
  • Data Sharing: We do not sell, trade, or transfer your Google user data to any third parties. The email data is transmitted directly via Google's secure API.

3. Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information. OAuth tokens and API credentials are stored securely as environment variables on our server and are never exposed to end users. Your personal data is stored in a secure database.

4. Third-Party Services

Our application uses Google Cloud services to facilitate email delivery. By using our service, you acknowledge that your email address will be processed by Google's infrastructure for the purpose of delivering transactional messages.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. If you wish to have your application data removed from our system, please contact our HR team.
